# hiroshima: ALOS PRISM tri-stereo of 23 Feb 2008, the Ota delta and its hills, 24 Nov 2026

1. **Input.** Forward, nadir, backward scenes (1B2R + RPC, 2.5 m, 45 s apart), cropped to a 12.2 x 10.8 km box: the delta, the port, the hills east and west, 31% sea and tidal rivers (masked from WorldCover). Clouds by the multi-view test: 173 / 64 / 104 ha in F / N / B, each over different ground. F-B converge at 53 deg (B/H 1.00, 2.5 m of height per pixel of disparity), the nadir pairs at 27 deg (B/H 0.50). The delivered RPCs count pixels from 1 (fixed) and disagree along track by 1.4 px (N-F) and 2.4 px (N-B).
2. **F-B alone (`fb`).** ASP MGM, 11 min: 89.4% of the land measured (no match 9.5%: behind tall buildings, steep slopes; cloud 1.1%). On flat open ground against the GSI laser DEM: as produced median -2.70 m, RMS 4.01, NMAD 1.42; anchored (shift ~2 m, uncertain by ~2 m; offset -2.71 m) RMS 2.89, NMAD 1.41, 95% within 4.78 m. All land as produced: median -0.67 m, RMS 6.96 m (roofs and canopy included: the GSI DEM is bare ground).
3. **With the nadir view (`fnb`, `fnb_w`).** Three pairs after a three-view bundle adjustment (N fixed), fused by inverse variance: 99.2% of the land measured, the cloud hole filled; same precision on flat ground (NMAD 1.46 m, RMS 2.91), but the level follows N: +6.11 m as produced. Each pair alone with the delivered RPCs: F-B -2.75, N-F +7.05, N-B -12.22 m. Subpixel mode 3 instead of 9: worse (NMAD 1.77 m) and 3x slower; mapprojecting onto fb's smoothed DSM first: 93.8% measured, NMAD 1.49 m, slopes worse.
4. **Where it fails.** Forested hills: NMAD 7 m on slopes of 15-30 deg, 9.5 m above 30; not the sun (every aspect alike) but the shape: ridges come out 5-6 m low, valleys 14 m high, a narrow wooded ridge on the island of Kanawajima 27-30 m low. The city is a surface, not ground: of the 10.7 km² GSI has below 1 m T.P. (21 km² below 2 m, behind the sea walls), the anchored DSM has 1.9 km² (IoU 0.09). Against Copernicus GLO-30 (30 m, anchored): built-up median +1.05 m, RMS 3.5; trees -4.3 m.
5. **Voxels.** 5.5M cubes of 5 m from the anchored tri-stereo DSM, the sea at 0 m T.P. (GSIGEO2011; the geoid is 33 m above the ellipsoid here).
6. **Only global inputs (`fnb_global`).** Heights above EGM2008, the level from Copernicus GLO-30 (a shift of 2.7 m and an offset of 7.22 m from 90,699 cells of flat open ground), the GSI laser DEM only as the check: -0.76 m against it on flat open ground, NMAD 1.46 m, RMS 3.02, 95% within 5.15 m. As produced the same cells are +6.45 m.
7. **Ground, water, routes (`fnb_global`).** The ground model (a robust opening of 210 m; under the dense downtown the open ground seen nearby; wooded hills keep the DSM): -0.46 m against GSI on flat built-up land, NMAD 1.16 m, where the DSM is +1.74 m, 2.54 m. A bathtub from the sea on the demo's 5 m cells, against the same on GSI (89.4 km² of land): IoU 0.81 at +3.44 m and 0.91-0.96 from 5 m; below about 3 m the model floods 2-10 times GSI's area (no sea walls, the level), and GSI's own bathtub floods 11.8 km² at a mean spring high tide, 6.2 km² of it through two cells of a river bank. Running to ground of 10 m or more from the land below 3.44 m: within 2 min of GSI's time for 88% of the people, 2-5 min longer for 12% (slopes with houses, flattened), on real high ground for 98%.
